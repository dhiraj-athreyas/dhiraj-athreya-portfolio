# Dhiraj Athreya Portfolio — Design Direction

## Ground-Truth Design Specification

This website follows the user-provided **Portfolio Website Design Specification — Stealth Mode Inspired** as the source of truth. The portfolio is conceived as an **AI Engineer × Systems Engineer × Research Lab × Technical Command Center**, rather than a conventional résumé site. Fidelity to the document’s restrained dark, architecture-first, cyberpunk-minimal direction takes priority over generic portfolio conventions.

## Chosen Approach: Stealth Systems Console

### Design Movement

**Cyberpunk Minimalism fused with a premium engineering dashboard.** The visual language takes its cues from technical schematics, terminal instrumentation, and calm high-performance interfaces instead of decorative sci-fi tropes.

### Core Principles

1. **Engineering credibility before decoration:** project architecture, measurable production outcomes, and technical roles carry the visual weight.
2. **Restrained atmosphere:** a near-black field, hairline blue rules, and quiet motion provide depth without visual noise.
3. **Asymmetric information hierarchy:** dense system metadata and large editorial typography share a modular layout.
4. **Scannable complexity:** accordions, labels, and system layers make advanced work easy to parse at a glance.

### Color Philosophy

The near-black `#030406` foundation signals focus and technical rigor. Deep navy surfaces organize information without becoming visually heavy; electric blue `#6EA8FF` is reserved for action, live data-flow, status, and moments of emphasis. Cool gray metadata deliberately recedes, producing an interface that feels measured rather than neon-heavy.

### Layout Paradigm

The site operates as an **engineering field report**. Full-width, rule-separated sections are organized around an offset content spine rather than a centered marketing stack. Hero content is split between identity and a live system-status console; projects unfold as horizontal system records; career history follows a vertical signal line.

### Signature Elements

1. A fine technical grid, atmospheric blue bloom, and low-contrast node graph behind the hero.
2. Monospaced section indexes, data labels, and terminal notation paired with editorial display type.
3. Animated signal paths and tiny status lamps that establish an operational, architecture-oriented feel.

### Interaction Philosophy

Every interaction should resemble inspecting a system rather than navigating a marketing page. Project rows expand to reveal architecture and outcomes; navigation moves precisely between system sections; controls have measured border illumination and a quick physical press response. Keyboard focus remains explicit and legible.

### Animation

Animations are understated and are disabled or minimized for reduced-motion preferences. Entrance reveals use short opacity and vertical-translation transitions (180–280 ms), while network paths and status pulses move slowly in the background. Hover transitions are limited to border brightness, surface lift, and text contrast; no parallax, rotating objects, or exaggerated glow.

### Typography System

**Space Grotesk** is the headline face: geometric, assertive, and technical without relying on the default Inter aesthetic. **IBM Plex Mono** is used for metadata, commands, dates, stack labels, and architecture. Headings use compact tracking at 600–700 weight; body copy is generous and readable; data labels are uppercase and tightly tracked.

### Brand Essence

**A systems-led AI/ML engineering portfolio for teams that need production-grade computer vision, speech, and generative AI.**

Personality: **precise, methodical, forward-looking.**

### Brand Voice

Headlines are direct, declarative, and oriented to engineering outcomes. CTAs use command-style verbs; microcopy behaves like useful instrumentation rather than filler.

> “Production AI systems, engineered for the real world.”

> “Inspect the systems →”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is `DHIRAJ//ATHREYA`, rendered with deliberate slash separation and monospaced system labeling. The graphic mark is an abstract **DA signal monogram**: two opposing angular channels joining at a glowing node, representing data flow and reliable system integration. The mark is icon-only, high-contrast, and legible at favicon scale.

### Signature Brand Color

**Signal Blue — `#6EA8FF`**: a controlled high-emphasis blue used for active states, live nodes, and navigation cues.

## Content & Data Rules

All achievements, technical stack entries, projects, education details, locations, and metrics must be drawn from the provided resume. Only observed engineering outcomes are displayed; no fabricated project links, testimonials, dates, performance claims, clients, or availability claims are introduced. The page is data-driven so future work can be added by extending content records rather than rewriting layout components.

## Style Decisions

- The **DA signal monogram** recurs as a subtle system motif through angular channels, joining nodes, signal paths, and the footer identity—not only as a header icon.
- `DHIRAJ//ATHREYA` is a technical wordmark with deliberate slash separation and monospaced precision, never generic navigation text.
- Every major section should include a visually dominant proof point—an observed metric, a system count, a production state, or a research-to-runtime result—so engineering impact balances the command-center atmosphere.
